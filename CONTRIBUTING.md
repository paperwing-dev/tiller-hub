# Contributing

Tiller uses an open-core model. The public core is Tiller Hub, which is source-available under the PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.0. The public core cannot be sold, hosted, distributed, or marketed as a competing product.

Separate add-ons are fair game. Anyone can build and sell add-ons that connect to or extend the public core, including CLIs, harnesses, runners, integrations, deployment tools, and management tools.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that:

- You have the right to submit the contribution.
- Your contribution is provided under the same license as the public Tiller core.
- Your contribution may be used, modified, and distributed as part of the public Tiller core under that license.

Submitting a contribution does not give the maintainer permission to sell, relicense, or include your contribution in a separate paid, hosted, enterprise, proprietary, or otherwise non-public Tiller offering. If the maintainer wants to use your contribution outside the public core license, they must get your separate written permission.

This does not prevent official or third-party add-ons from depending on the public core under the public core license.

The maintainer may decline contributions for any reason, including product direction, maintenance cost, changes that blur the core/add-on boundary, or changes that would make the public core compete with Tiller's commercial roadmap.

Please keep pull requests focused and explain the user-facing behavior change. For larger changes, open an issue or discussion first so the product boundary is clear before implementation.
